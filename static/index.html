Research Projects

Bachelor's Thesis (TFG) Active / Completed

Quantum Digital Twin of Glycine

Development of a three-dimensional quantum digital twin of the glycine molecule (C₂H₅NO₂) simulated under Variational Quantum Eigensolver (**VQE** - Variational Quantum Eigensolver) algorithms. The model simulates a **CAS(10,10)** active space in **20 qubits** utilizing the fermionic-inspired **cfSim** (Current Fermionic Simulation) ansatz. The project validates how cfSim's physical constraints on particle number conservation and spin parity prevent Hartree-Fock energy disruptions associated with classical noise gates (standard CNOTs in Hardware Efficient Ansatzes), achieving optimal convergence stability on noisy NISQ architectures of IBM Quantum.
