Quantum Space Explanation
Standard Molecular Model Shows all chemical bonds of glycine. The C (grey), O (red), N (blue), and H (white) atoms are classically simulated in the Hartree-Fock mean field.
Deprotonation Dynamics
Glycine-Water Interaction Simulates the carboxyl O-H bond cleavage in the presence of a water molecule. The proton (H⁺) transfers to H₂O, forming a hydronium (H₃O⁺) cation and glycine carboxylate (Gly-COO⁻).

Excitation Mechanics
Fermionic Exchange (cfSim) The VQE utilizes fSim gates mapping the hopping terms aj†ai + ai†aj ≡ |0i1j⟩⟨1i0j| + |1i0j⟩⟨0i1j| that model physical charge transport while preserving particle number conservation. This corresponds to the qubit state transition |1i0j⟩ → |0i1j⟩.
